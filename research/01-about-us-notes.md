# AVVVS research notes: About Us page

Source: https://avvvs.com/about-us/ (read 2026-11-07)

## Facts stated on the page (verbatim-grounded)
- Founded **1921** in Kozhikode (Calicut), Malabar, Kerala.
- Founder: **Kaloor Neelakandan Vaidyar**. The page also spells it Neelakantan and "Sri. K. Neelakandan Vaidyar (Founder)".
- He was "personally blessed and advised" by **Sree Narayana Guru** to establish a patient care facility. It began as a pharmacy preparing and dispensing Ayurveda medicines.
- Successor: his son **K. N. Ramadas Vaidyar** (also spelled "Ramdas"). The page notes his presence in Kozhikode's socio-literary circles and his satire.
- Present management: **Dr Manoj Kaloor** and **Reeja Manoj**.
- Manufactures, supplies, exports and retails classical and patent (proprietary) medicines. The clinical arm became **AVVVS Speciality Clinics**.
- **GMP-certified factory** in the city suburbs. Equipment named: capsulation, blister packing, ointment manufacture and filling, cosmetic product manufacturing, plus traditional mechanised units.
- Exports named: Russia, Austria, Slovenia, Colombia (spelled "Columbia" on the site).
- Founding idea: social service.
